# GTA 6 Map: Leonida & Vice City

GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of **Leonida**, Rockstar's take on Florida, centred on **Vice City** (based on Miami).

## Interactive GTA 6 maps

- [MapGenie: Official Grand Theft Auto 6 Map](https://mapgenie.io/grand-theft-auto-6): filter collectibles, activities and points of interest
- [IGN GTA 6 Guide](https://www.ign.com/wikis/gta-6): map and location pages
- [Vice Keys Leonida Atlas](https://getvicekeys.com/atlas): Search named community landmarks with approximate positions and open related location walkthroughs.

## How big is the GTA 6 map?

| Claim | Who says it | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| About **twice** the size of GTA 5's map | Attributed to Rockstar in coverage | ⚠️ Reported comparison |
| About **3×** Red Dead Redemption 2's playable area | Attributed to Rockstar in coverage | ⚠️ Reported comparison |
| ~83 km² of land (~1.7× GTA 5's 48 km²) | Community measurement | ⚠️ Estimate |
| ~151.8 km² (58.6 sq mi) | Community measurement | ⚠️ Estimate |

Rockstar hasn't published an official square-kilometre figure, and community measurements disagree, so treat the numbers as estimates. Explainer: [GTA Intel](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-map-size-explained).
